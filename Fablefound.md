# Fable review findings — math, scale and incompatible-option issues (continued)

Static code review (nothing was run). Review only; no code was changed.
Items 1–22 were delivered in the chat and are not repeated here; numbering continues from 23.

| # | Short description | Priority | Problem | File |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 23 | B4B lift grabbers stick into the child-bin field | High | B4B grabbers grow inward from the inner mating wall by 1.5–3.5 mm, 8 mm below the rim. Child bins are meant to fill that field edge to edge and stand up to the rim minus 0.5–2 mm headroom, so the grabber sits inside the space a perimeter child bin occupies and stops it seating. The grabber check only looks at wall length and rim/skirt clearance, never at the child field. | organizer_b4b.py (`validate_b4b_lift_grabbers`, `make_b4b_lift_grabbers`) |
| 24 | Stacking + tall centre parts collide with the lid plug or upper bin foot | High | Only parts within 2 mm of a wall are held below the connector strip. A bore, pocket, post or steps in the middle of the floor may rise to the rim (a bore's default height reaches it). With stacking on, the lid plug or the bin above drops 3 mm into the mouth, so those parts are hit. There is no plug-depth keep-out anywhere in the interior-part checks. | organizer_inserts/_assembly.py (`build_features`), organizer_stack.py (`STACK_PLUG_DEPTH`) |
| 25 | B4B height in the drawer is the child height, not the case | High | A B4B is logged with Z equal to the child-bin height. The printed case is floor (0.6–2.8) + child height + headroom (0.5–2) + lid plate (1.6–2.4) tall, plus 2 mm of stacking pegs, so the drawer's "too tall" check understates it by roughly 3–9 mm. Companion to #3 (footprint). | organizer_app.py (`inventory_bin_record`), organizer_drawer.py (`stack_part_height`) |
| 26 | B4B "Lid Only" + "Tight" snugness gives a lid with no location | Medium | The locating skirt is limited to headroom minus 0.15 mm and is dropped entirely below 0.4 mm. At 0.5 mm snugness that is 0.35 mm, so no skirt is built. With Lid Only there are no hinges or latches either, so the lid is a flat plate resting on the rim, free to slide off. The UI offers this combination without comment. | organizer_b4b.py (`_skirt_lap`, `make_b4b_lid`) |
| 27 | Bin auto-growth searches up to 800 mm before failing | Low | The layout expander grows the bin one step at a time up to 100 units (800 mm), but the engine refuses anything over 350 mm, so every trial past 344 mm silently fails and the loop keeps going until 800 before reporting "will not fit even in a very large bin". Wasted work and a misleading message. | wavefinity_web.py (`expand_layout_payload`) |
| 28 | Bore, pocket, post and cradle can be taller than the bin with no error | Medium | Divider, slot, steps, scoop, nest and text all refuse a height that passes the rim. Bore, pocket, post and cradle never compare their height to the bin height, so a typed 40 mm pocket in a 20 mm bin, or a 30 mm-diameter tool cradle in a 10 mm bin, builds a part that stands above the rim. The 3D preview shows it; export does not stop it. | organizer_inserts/_bore.py (`_bore_grid`), _pocket.py (`build_pocket`), _post.py (`build_post`), _cradle.py (`build_cradle`) |
| 29 | Pocket default height on short bins is above the rim by design | Medium | For a bin 20 mm or shorter the pocket's default height is the full bin height, measured from the floor top, so the pocket top lands 0.6 mm above the rim (1.2 mm on a removable insert). Combined with #28 nothing catches it. On taller bins the default is 40% of the bin, which is fine. | organizer_inserts/_pocket.py (`pocket_defaults`) |
| 30 | Default scoop is refused on bins about 22 mm or shorter | Low | A new scoop rises 60% of the wall height and touches the wall, so it must stay below rim minus 8.4 mm for the connector. On a 20 mm bin that is 12.2 mm of scoop against an 11.6 mm limit, so the default part errors the moment it is added instead of being clamped to a legal depth. | organizer_inserts/_scoop.py (`scoop_settings`), organizer_inserts/_assembly.py (`build_features` wall check) |
| 31 | Stacking lids for different wall thicknesses share one filename | Low | The lid file is named "Stack Lid X x Y" with no wall thickness, but the lid's plug is sized from the cavity, which depends on the wall. Two lid-stack bins of the same footprint with 1.2 mm and 2.0 mm walls write the same lid file and the second silently overwrites the first. The bin file itself does carry the wall in its name. | organizer_app.py (`stack_lid_filename`) |
| 32 | B4B stacking is invisible to the drawer planner | Low | A B4B can be printed stackable (pegs and recesses), but its inventory row records stacking as "none" and the planner only stacks lid/direct bins, so stackable B4B cases can never be stacked in the Space view and their peg height is not counted. | organizer_app.py (`inventory_bin_record`), organizer_drawer.py (`_build_stacks`), web/drawer-model.js (`DL.stackable`) |
| 33 | Photo scale is hard-wired to US Letter paper | Medium | The photo outline is scaled by mapping the detected sheet to 215.9 × 279.4 mm. There is no paper-size choice, so a part photographed on A4 (210 × 297) comes out about 3% too wide and 6% too short; a Snug Holder cut from that will not fit the part. The sheet detector accepts either sheet without complaint. | photo_nest.py (`LETTER_WIDTH_MM`, `LETTER_HEIGHT_MM`, `perspective_transform_inputs`) |
| 34 | Hand-added stackable bins double-count the foot | Low | The "Add a bin by hand" form asks for "Closed height, lid included", but the planner treats inventory Z as the module height and adds the 1 or 3 mm foot on top. A hand-added direct-snap bin entered at its true 43 mm is planned as 46 mm, and its stack pitch is 3 mm too big. Generated bins log the module height, so only hand-added rows are affected. | web/drawer-panel.js (`#dl-add-z`), organizer_drawer.py (`stack_part_height`) |
| 35 | "Design a bin for it" ignores the stacking foot | Low | Filling a drawer gap sets the bin height to the drawer's max height, but if the current design is a stackable bin the printed part is 1 or 3 mm taller than that, so the bin it hands back is already too tall for the drawer it was sized for. | web/drawer-panel.js (design-for-gap handler, ~line 431) |
| 36 | Lock bumps intrude into the "usable inside" rectangle; removable inserts must force past them | Medium | The usable rectangle is the cavity's innermost line. Half the lock bumps sit exactly on that line (the inward wave crests) and stand 0.35 mm further in, 4–5 mm below the rim. A removable insert plate is only 0.2 mm inside the cavity, so it interferes with every inward-crest bump by about 0.15 mm on all four walls and has to be snapped past roughly ten bumps per long wall to go in or come out. The same 0.35 mm is missing from the advertised usable width in that band. | organizer_engine.py (`usable_inside`, `_wall_lock_paths`, `LOCK_PROTRUSION`), organizer_inserts/_core.py (`INSERT_CLEARANCE`), organizer_inserts/_assembly.py (`insert_footprint`) |
| 37 | Editor endpoints validate stacked bins against the wrong height | Low | The preview and export build a lid-stack bin from a body shortened by the lid rise (about 1–2 mm) and a direct-stack body 3 mm taller, but the add/draft/fit/grow endpoints use the typed module height. A hand-typed part height that passes "Add" can fail in the preview and at export on a lid-stack bin. | wavefinity_web.py (`default_feature_payload`, `draft_payload`, `apply_feature_payload`, `feature_fit_payload`, `expand_layout_payload`) |
| 38 | Rim label or lift grabbers refuse every default divider | High | A new divider's zone is the whole floor (so Quantity can spread walls evenly) even though its walls cover only a thin strip. The keep-out checks for the rim label ledge and lift grabbers compare the *zone*, not the covered strip, so any bin with a rim label or grabbers rejects a default divider with "overlaps the rim label ledge" no matter where the wall actually stands. Auto-placement uses the covered strip and lets it in; validation then throws it out. | organizer_app.py (`validate_customization_clearance`, `preview_geometry` reserved-zone loop) |
