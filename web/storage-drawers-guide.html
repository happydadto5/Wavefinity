Storage Drawers — assembly & print guide

This one page covers every Storage Drawers cabinet. Wavefinity saves each part as its own 3MF file, already turned into the orientation that fits your printer. Nothing needs screws, glue or tools for standard assembly.

1. Which part is which

Every file starts with your Space name, followed by the part name. Match the part name to this map:

Left and Right are named by the cabinet's front. If you are unsure, hold the part against the cabinet: a side's spring arm belongs at the front-top edge, and its drawer rails face inward.

2. Assembly order

  1. Base: place the Cabinet Base flat with its rails up.
  2. Sides: slide the bottom dovetail of each side into its base rail from the back, pushing forward until it stops. The sides stand upright and parallel.
  3. Rear: lower the rear panel straight down from above into the vertical grooves at the back of both sides. It should drop in under its own weight, with no pressing. It rests in the pocket in the base.
  4. Drawers: push each drawer in straight from the front (see step 4).
  5. Top: slide the Cabinet Top on from the back toward the front over the two top dovetails. Its front channels pass over the spring arms, which click when the Top is fully seated.
  6. Stacking: if you made Stack Pegs, press one into each top socket to stack another cabinet above.

3. Removing the Top

Take out the top drawer first. Reach in under the Top at the front corner on each side and press the small spring arm toward the outer wall with a fingertip. While the arm is pressed, slide the Top backward until it is free. Nothing needs to be bent hard; if an arm will not move, check that you are pressing toward the wall, not upward.

If your top drawer is short (about 10 mm or less), your fingertip will not fit in the opening — use a thin tool instead, such as a 2 mm hex key or a bamboo skewer. Work one side at a time: slide the tool in at the front corner until it touches the spring arm, press the arm toward the outer wall, and while holding it, push the Top backward a few millimetres with your other hand. Repeat on the other side until the Top is free.

4. Drawers

Drawers push in and pull out in a straight line, and stop exactly flush with the cabinet front — they cannot be pushed in behind it. There is a light click as a drawer passes its catch — do not lift the drawer. If it feels stiff, choose a looser Drawer fit in the Drawers section, save the cabinet again and reprint only the drawers. Avoid Tight on a first print: at that setting the drawer catch has almost no spare travel, so a slightly oversized print can jam instead of clicking.

5. Printing

  • Load each file as it is. The orientation Wavefinity chose is the one it checked against your printer's build volume, so do not rotate it.
  • Use your normal material profile for a rigid part.
  • Use at least 3 perimeters (walls) and about 15% infill.
  • Leave supports off. Pull handles normally print without supports as small horizontal tubes; the underside may show minor roughness. Localized support under just a pull may be used where the slicer supports it.
  • If a part will not fit your printer, open Printer Settings… and check the build volume, or reduce the cabinet size or drawer count.

Wavefinity does not prescribe temperatures or speeds; use your printer's usual settings for the material.
