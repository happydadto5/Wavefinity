Welcome to Wavefinity

Wavefinity designs 3D-printed bins sized to the space they live in.

  1. Set up a spaceA drawer you already have, or a box Wavefinity designs for you. Each space gets a name, its inside size and its own folder.
  2. Design bins to fill itEvery bin you generate is saved in the space's folder.
  3. Keep track of what's insideThe space's inventory file lists every bin, and the Space tab shows where each one sits.
